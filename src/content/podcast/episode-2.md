## Episode Summary

Hosts Amarachi, Ruth, and Joseph return for the second episode of *In The Pews*, diving deep into what it truly means to be intimate with God — not just knowing about Him, but knowing Him personally.

## Topics Discussed

### Intimacy with God

What does a genuine, close relationship with God actually look like day to day? The hosts unpack the difference between religious routine and real intimacy — the kind that transforms how you think, speak, and live.

### Prayer and Devotion

The group explores practical devotional habits and why consistency matters more than intensity. Real talk on the struggle to maintain a prayer life when life gets busy, and how to get back on track when you drift.

### Knowing God Personally

Faith can become inherited rather than personal. The hosts challenge listeners to move beyond their parents' or church's relationship with God and pursue their own encounter — asking questions, seeking answers, and wrestling with Scripture.

### Faith in Everyday Life

How does intimacy with God show up in Monday-morning decisions? The conversation connects personal devotion to real-life choices — relationships, ambitions, and how young Christians navigate a world that often pulls the other way.
