Jesus House Jamaica is pleased to announce a formal partnership with the **Kingston Community Outreach Initiative (KCOI)** — a coalition of churches and community organisations working to serve the most vulnerable residents of Kingston.

## What This Partnership Means

Beginning in April 2026, Jesus House members will join monthly outreach days in the Arnett Gardens, Trench Town, and Denham Town communities. Each outreach day includes:

- **Hot meals** prepared and distributed by our hospitality team
- **Basic medical screening** — blood pressure, blood sugar, and vision checks — provided by volunteer medical professionals in our congregation
- **Prayer and pastoral care** for those who request it
- **Children's activities** run by our youth team

## Why This Matters

The Gospel is good news for the whole person. Jesus healed the sick, fed the hungry, and restored the dignity of those whom society had discarded — and then He told us to do the same.

This partnership is not charity in the condescending sense. It is neighbour-love. It is the church being the church.

## How You Can Be Involved

We need people with time, skills, and a servant's heart. Whether you are a medical professional, a cook, a musician, or simply someone willing to show up — there is a place for you.

Speak to any of our pastoral team after any service, or email us at **jesushousekgn@hotmail.com** with the subject line *Outreach*.
