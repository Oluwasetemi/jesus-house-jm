Three days. Hundreds of young people. And the unmistakable presence of God.

The Jesus House Jamaica Youth Conference 2023 exceeded every expectation we had going in. Here is a record of what God did — so that we do not forget, and so that faith rises for what He will do next.

## Day One: Arrival and Hunger

The first evening set the tone. Worship ran long — not because we planned it, but because no one wanted to leave the atmosphere. The speaker's message on identity ("You are not who the world says you are") landed with unusual power, and the altar call drew young people who had never responded publicly before.

**By the numbers:** 340 registered. 28 first-time salvations on night one alone.

## Day Two: The Breakthrough Session

Saturday morning is when things shifted. During the 6am prayer session — which we expected few to attend — the room was packed. Something broke in the spirit. Testimonies flowed through the day: a young man healed of chronic back pain, a young woman reconciled with her estranged father after a spontaneous phone call during a ministry session.

The afternoon workshops on relationships, calling, and mental health were standing-room only.

## Day Three: Commissioning

The final morning was commissioning. Young people left not just encouraged, but *sent* — with specific assignments, prayer partners, and a renewed sense of purpose in their local communities.

## What We Carry Forward

Youth Conference 2023 reminded us that young people in Jamaica are hungry for genuine encounter with God. They are not looking for entertainment — they are looking for truth, community, and a reason to believe their lives matter.

We are already planning for the next one. Watch this space.
