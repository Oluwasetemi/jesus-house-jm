Prayer is the engine of the Christian life — and yet it is often the first thing to slip when life gets busy. If you have ever found yourself going days without meaningful prayer, you are not alone. Here are five strategies that work.

## 1. Attach Prayer to Something You Already Do

Habit researchers call this "habit stacking." Instead of carving out a new time, anchor prayer to an existing routine — your morning coffee, your commute, the moment before you eat. When you do the anchor habit, prayer follows automatically.

## 2. Start Smaller Than You Think You Should

Five minutes of focused prayer beats thirty minutes of distracted guilt. Begin with five minutes and let it grow. The goal in the early days is consistency, not duration.

## 3. Pray Out Loud

Silent prayer is valid, but speaking aloud engages your whole self and makes it much harder for your mind to wander. Many people find that praying aloud transforms their prayer life overnight.

## 4. Keep a Prayer List

A simple list — on paper or on your phone — prevents the "I don't know what to pray for" paralysis. Write down names, situations, and requests. Review it. Update it. Celebrate when God answers.

## 5. Join a Prayer Community

Jesus House holds **Monday Morning Prayer** and **Wednesday Prayer Meeting** precisely because corporate prayer multiplies faith. There is something about praying with others that pushes you past your own spiritual ceiling.
